\begin{abstract}
We propose a non-perturbative method for computing the renormalization
constants of  generic
composite operators. This method is intended to reduce some systematic errors,
which are present when one tries to obtain physical predictions
 from the matrix
elements of lattice operators. We also present the results of a calculation
of the renormalization constants of several two-fermion operators, obtained,
with our method, by
numerical simulation of $QCD$, on a $16^3 \times 32$ lattice, at $\beta=6.0$.
The results of this simulation are encouraging, and further applications
to four-fermion operators and to the heavy quark effective theory are
proposed.
\end{abstract}
