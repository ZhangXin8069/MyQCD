\begin{abstract}
We propose a new locally smeared operator product expansion to
decompose nonlocal operators in terms of a basis of smeared
operators. The smeared operator product expansion formally connects
nonperturbative matrix elements
determined numerically using lattice field
theory to matrix elements of nonlocal operators in the continuum. These
nonperturbative matrix
elements do not suffer
from power-divergent mixing on the lattice, which significantly
complicates calculations of quantities such as the moments of parton
distribution functions, provided the smearing scale is kept
fixed in the continuum limit. The presence of this smearing scale complicates
the connection to the Wilson coefficients of the standard operator product
expansion and
requires the construction of a suitable formalism. We demonstrate
the feasibility of our approach with examples in real
scalar field theory.
\end{abstract}
