Hadrons in lattice QCD are usually created employing smeared interpolators.
We introduce a new quark smearing that
allows us to maintain small statistical errors and good overlaps of hadronic
wave functions with the respective ground states, also at high spatial
momenta. The method is successfully tested for the pion and the nucleon
at a pion mass $m_{\pi}\approx 295\units{MeV}$ and momenta as high as
2.8\units{GeV}. We compare the results obtained to dispersion
relations and suggest further optimizations.
