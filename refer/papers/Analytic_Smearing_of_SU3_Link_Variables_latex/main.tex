\begin{abstract}
An analytic method of smearing link variables in lattice QCD is proposed and
tested.  The differentiability of the smearing scheme with respect to the
link variables permits the use of modern Monte Carlo updating methods based
on molecular dynamics evolution for gauge-field actions constructed using
such smeared links.  In examining the smeared mean plaquette and the static
quark-antiquark potential, no degradation in effectiveness is observed as
compared to link smearing methods currently in use, although an increased
sensitivity to the smearing parameter is found.
\end{abstract}
