\begin{abstract}
We present the first determination of the $x$-dependent pion gluon distribution from lattice QCD using the pseudo-PDF approach.
We use lattice ensembles with 2+1+1 flavors of highly improved staggered quarks (HISQ), generated by MILC Collaboration, at two lattice spacings $a\approx 0.12$ and 0.15~fm and three pion masses $M_\pi\approx 220$, 310 and 690~MeV.
We use clover fermions for the valence action and momentum smearing to achieve pion boost momentum up to 2.29~GeV.
We find that the dependence of the pion gluon parton distribution on lattice spacing and pion mass is mild.
We compare our results from the lightest pion mass ensemble with the determination by JAM and xFitter global fits.
\end{abstract}
