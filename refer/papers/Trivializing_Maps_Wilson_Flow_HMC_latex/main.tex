\begin{abstract}
\noindent
In lattice gauge theory, there exist field transformations
that map the theory to the trivial one, where the
basic field variables
are completely decoupled from one another.
Such maps can be constructed systematically
by integrating certain flow equations
in field space.
The construction is worked out
in some detail and it is proposed to
combine the Wilson flow
(which generates approximately trivializing maps
for the Wilson gauge action)
with the HMC simulation algorithm
in order to improve the efficiency of
lattice QCD simulations.
\end{abstract}
